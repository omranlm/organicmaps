Syria Organic Maps (خرائط سوريا) is an offline map and GPS navigation app for Syria, Arabic first, free and without ads. The OpenStreetMap Syria community makes it from the open-source Organic Maps app, with local OpenStreetMap data for Syria. In Arabic, people also search for it as خرايط سوريا or خريطة سوريا.

<b>Offline:</b> the whole map of Syria downloads on the first launch. After that, search, navigation and voice guidance work without any connection, which saves data and battery.

<b>Navigation in Arabic:</b> routes for driving, walking and cycling, with turn-by-turn voice guidance in Arabic or English.

<b>Fast search:</b> find cities, neighbourhoods, streets and shops in Damascus, Aleppo, Homs, Hama, Latakia, Tartus, Deir ez-Zor and every governorate.

<b>Record your trips:</b> record the route of a trip with one tap. It is saved on your phone and uploaded when you are online. Your recordings help the OpenStreetMap Syria community add missing roads to the map. Sign in with your phone number, or record without one.

<b>Live location:</b> share your location while driving, only when you turn it on. It stops by itself when you stop moving.

<b>Share places:</b> send any place as a link that opens the app right on it. People without the app see the place on a map.

<b>Up-to-date maps:</b> map updates for Syria arrive every month, without updating the app.

<b>Privacy first:</b> no ads and no tracking. Nothing is sent to our server before you agree, and you can delete your account and all its data from Settings at any time.

<b>From Organic Maps:</b> bookmarks and tracks (KML, GPX, GeoJSON), contour lines and hiking trails, night mode, and OpenStreetMap editing.

Syria Organic Maps is free and open source. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors. This app is not officially affiliated with the Organic Maps project.
